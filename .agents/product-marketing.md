# sonu — Product Marketing Context

## Product
sonu is a chess training app that pairs Stockfish engine play with an AI coach that explains every move. Built for the browser, desktop and mobile.

## Tagline
Train chess with an AI coach that explains every move

## One-liner
Play Stockfish at your level. Get hints when stuck. Ask the AI why any move works.

## Target Audience
- Adult chess improvers (1200-2000 Elo) who want to get better but don't have a human coach
- Casual players who want to understand why moves are good or bad
- Self-learners who prefer Socratic teaching over engine lines

## Key Differentiators
- AI coach in two modes: Socratic (asks guiding questions) and Assistant (gives clear answers)
- Pay-per-use model — no subscriptions, only pay for AI answers
- Voice conversations with the AI coach
- Stockfish with adaptive think time — scales difficulty to your level
- Cross-device sync with Google sign-in

## Brand Voice
- Direct and clear — no fluff, no hype
- Educational but not pedantic
- Confident but not arrogant
- Speaks to people who take chess seriously but aren't grandmasters

## Visual Identity
- Warm cream canvas (#faf9f5), coral primary (#cc785c), dark navy surfaces (#181715)
- Display headings in Cormorant Garamond, body in Inter
- Dark cosmic-themed backgrounds
- Minimal, editorial feel
